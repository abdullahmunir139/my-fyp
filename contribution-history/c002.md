# c002 — AgriPulse Proposal Rewritten For Defensibility

## What was done

Rewrote the FYP proposal for **AgriPulse** so that every claim in it survives
adversarial review by a supervisor, an LLM, or a domain expert. The previous
version was structurally correct but contained claims that could not be
defended, and several of them contradicted the document's own contents.

Thirteen claims were audited against the locked scope of record
(`chat-history/outputs/A4.md`) and against what actually exists in the
repository. The substantive corrections:

- **"0 external dependencies" was false** — the proposal's own tools table
  listed sixteen. Restated precisely as **zero runtime network dependencies**
  (no CDN, no external API, no font host, no telemetry, no runtime download),
  with the sixteen free build-time packages named honestly. This was the most
  immediately fatal line in the document.
- **Every quantity was relabelled** as a published figure, a calculated result,
  or a gated target, and never two at once. "420 reference rows" had been
  written as an achievement; nothing is built. It is now a week-1 target with a
  pass/fail gate.
- **A crop-sourcing rule was added**: a crop appears in the dropdown only if
  its cost and yield rows have a named downloadable public source. Crops
  without sourced data are visibly disabled rather than filled with guesses,
  and shipping fewer than five crops is a planned outcome rather than a failure.
- **Cotton was separated from the shipped data.** Cotton is the published
  validation reference for the SFRI method — 2.35 bags Urea and 1.04 bags DAP
  per acre, PCPA Cotton Book — and is explicitly **not** one of the crops whose
  data ships. The previous draft blurred this line.
- **Disease diagnosis was removed from the Problem Statement** and stated once
  as a real problem that is explicitly out of scope, with the reason. The
  document previously promised four problems and then admitted it solved three.
- **A Deployment and Use section was added**, answering the strongest attack on
  the project: what actually ships, how a farmer reaches it, and what is not
  claimed — no hosting, no public accounts, no live price feed, no government
  integration, no field trial.
- **Two technical errors were corrected**: a `ppm` to `kg/ha` conversion that
  was wrongly attributed to the farmer's area, and an unverifiable Lighthouse
  performance target replaced by a measurement the student can actually show.
- **Competitor analysis was preserved**, per the user's judgement that it was
  sound. Only the unverifiable third-party reach figures were removed; the
  capability comparison and the structural gap statement remain.
- **Present-tense product voice was replaced** with conditional, planned
  voice, with one explicit line stating that the project has not started.

The official template's section order was followed exactly, and the section
content-types the user specified in Q5, Q6, Q7 and Q8 were preserved: the Q6
four-bullet problem shape, and the Q8 structure of one combined Aim and
Objective heading with a 5-point aim block and 6-point objective block, Scope
as one paragraph plus six descriptive points with no sub-headings, and
Functional and Non-Functional Requirements as plain numbering at twelve each.

Six specialist agentsets from `specilized-agents-skills-from-agency-agents`
were applied read-only — Proposal Strategist, Academic Statistician, Technical
Writer, Senior Project Manager, Product Manager, and Software Architect. No
sibling repository was written to.
