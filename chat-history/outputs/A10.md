# A10 — Summary

## 1. Project Summary

**AgriPulse AI** is a Final Year Project (FYP) conceived as a gap-only agricultural intelligence platform for smallholder farmers. The project deliberately avoids generic AI branding and focuses on delivering actionable, data-driven insights through a structured 5-module scope.

### Core Identity
- **Name:** AgriPulse AI
- **Type:** Gap-only agricultural advisory platform
- **Target users:** Smallholder farmers
- **Avoids:** Generic "AI" naming, broad model-training ambitions, and over-scoped feature creep

### Architecture & Scope
- **Data layer:** Aggregates from 8 external data sources (weather, market prices, soil, crop calendars, pest databases, government schemes, satellite imagery, local mandi rates)
- **Processing:** Rule-based gap analysis and SFRI-style scoring rather than deep learning models
- **Modules:** 5 locked modules — Weather Intelligence, Market Price Tracker, Crop Advisory, Pest/Disease Alert, Government Scheme Matcher
- **Boundary:** Excludes Leaf Check and Reports modules (cut in Q4)

### Documentation Progress
- **Proposal:** Built from official college template, includes 27 tools, 51 Functional Requirements, 20 Non-Functional Requirements
- **Problem statements:** 4 distinct problems compressed into 4 one-line bullets
- **Proposal sections:** Combined aim-objective blocks, scope paragraph plus six bullet points, paste-ready paragraphs for aim, objectives, and scope
- **Tables:** 12 FR and 12 NFR presented in equal two-column lists

### Design Artifacts
- **HHD:** High-level data flow diagrams in Mermaid
- **LLD:** Low-level design with module boundaries
- **Wireframes:** Seven ASCII wireframes covering all five modules
- **ER model:** Database schema planned but not yet finalized in deliverables
- **Token plan:** Development timeline broken into token-efficient iterations

### Constraints & Decisions
- **No model training:** Project uses existing APIs and rule engines, avoiding custom ML training
- **Competitor landscape:** 13 existing players audited; 12 out of 16 problem factors covered by current solutions
- **Renaming:** Dropped "AI" from visible branding to reduce expectation mismatch
- **USP:** Gap-only coverage for under-served farmer segments with offline-first mobile design
